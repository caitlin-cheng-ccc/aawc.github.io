Pelvic Floor Therapy

What Is Emsella?

Emsella is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive chair that uses High-Intensity Focused Electromagnetic (HIFEM) technology to stimulate deep pelvic floor muscles — the same muscles targeted by Kegel exercises, but far more effectively.

A single 28-minute session delivers thousands of supramaximal pelvic floor contractions, the equivalent of roughly 11,000 Kegels — far more than most people could ever perform on their own, and without the guesswork of whether you're doing them correctly.

What Does This Treatment Address?

Emsella is most commonly used to treat:

  • Stress urinary incontinence (leaking when you laugh, cough, sneeze, or exercise)
  • Urge incontinence and overactive bladder symptoms
  • Pelvic floor weakness following childbirth
  • Reduced core stability and sexual wellness concerns tied to pelvic floor strength

It's a great option for anyone who wants a non-surgical, fully clothed, no-downtime way to strengthen the pelvic floor — no matter what stage of life brought them here.

How Do Emsella Treatments Work?

01
