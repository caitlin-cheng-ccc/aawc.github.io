Body Contouring

Body contouring uses advanced, non-invasive technology to sculpt, tighten, and tone — helping you achieve the shape you've worked for without incisions or downtime.

Now available at our Abington, PA location

Why Choose Body Contouring

No incisions, no anesthesia, no recovery period — just minimal downtime, quick sessions, and real results that build gradually and look natural, so you can return to your normal routine immediately.

Beyond the physical changes, clients consistently tell us how much their confidence improves once they see and feel the results — a firmer core, a smoother silhouette, and a body that finally matches how they feel on the inside.

Build Muscle · Burn Fat

Emsculpt NEO

Emsculpt NEO is built for clients who want both muscle tone and fat reduction in one treatment. It combines HIFEM+ energy with radiofrequency heating to simultaneously build muscle fibers and destroy fat cells in the treated area, something no gym routine can replicate on its own. Most clients see optimal results in as few as four sessions.

Tighten · Smooth · Refine

T Shape 2

Where Emsculpt NEO focuses on muscle and fat, T Shape 2 takes on a different job: tightening loose or sagging skin and smoothing the appearance of cellulite. Using radiofrequency, vacuum therapy, and infrared light, it stimulates natural collagen production so skin looks firmer and more refined as it heals.

The Complete Approach

Combining Both Treatments

For clients who want the most complete transformation, we often recommend pairing Emsculpt NEO with T Shape 2. While Emsculpt NEO builds muscle and burns fat beneath the surface, T Shape 2 tightens and refines the skin above it — together, giving you a more sculpted, smoother, and more defined result than either treatment could achieve alone.

Body contouring is a category of non-surgical treatments designed to sculpt, tone, and tighten the body by targeting fat, muscle, and skin — without incisions, anesthesia, or downtime. At AAWC, we use Emsculpt NEO and T Shape 2 to address these goals from every angle.

Emsculpt NEO combines HIFEM+ (High-Intensity Focused Electromagnetic) energy with radiofrequency heating in a single applicator. The HIFEM+ energy causes rapid, supramaximal muscle contractions to build muscle, while the RF heat works to eliminate fat cells in the same session.
