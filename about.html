About Us

Abington Aesthetics & Wellness Center was built on a simple idea: exceptional care and advanced technology shouldn't be out of reach. Get to know the people and the purpose behind every treatment we offer.

Proudly serving Abington, PA and the surrounding community

Our Mission

At Abington Aesthetics & Wellness Center, our mission is simple: help every client look in the mirror and feel proud of what they see. We believe real confidence comes from feeling comfortable in your own skin, not chasing an unrealistic ideal.

That's why we combine advanced, non-invasive technology with a genuinely caring team — so every treatment plan is built around your goals, your body, and your life, not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Meet the Team

Dr. Mike Cheng

Clinical Director

Dr. Wai-Wen "Mike" Cheng is a board-certified chiropractic physician with over 23 plus years of clinical experience in chiropractic care, physical medicine, natural pain relief, and modern wellness solutions. He strives to help patients feel and look their best by offering FDA-cleared, medical-grade body sculpting technology that offers real patient results. He and his team aim to bring the best and latest technology to help his patients achieve their health and aesthetic goals.

Jessica Spears

Aesthetics & Wellness Specialist

With more than 10 years of experience working alongside Dr. Mike, Jessica brings a deeply personal approach to aesthetics and wellness. Her passion is the T-Shape 2, a technology she loves for its versatility and ability to address a wide range of aesthetic and wellness goals. Jessica is known for the care and attention she gives each client, taking the time to understand their individual goals and creating a personalized treatment experience from beginning to end. Her own wellness journey has shaped her passion for helping others feel confident, empowered, and comfortable in their own skin. For Jessica, the greatest reward is seeing not only her clients' results, but the confidence that comes along with them.

Stephanie Wang

Aesthetic Technician

Stephanie is our resident expert in Emsculpt Neo and T-shape treatments. With a deep understanding of muscle physiology and body composition, she tailors every session to maximize results and comfort for each individual client.

Allyson Asnes

Aesthetic Consultant

Allyson leads our sales team, helping clients find the right treatment plan for their goals and budget. Her warm, straightforward approach makes it easy to ask questions and feel confident about every decision.
